# Investigation and process reports

`reports/` contains outputs and supporting evidence from specific audits, investigations, migrations, validations and repairs. Reports can remain useful review material or become historical evidence; they are not the authoritative home for general project policy. Use [project documentation](../docs/README.md) for maintained specifications, workflows, decisions and issue/question statuses.

For policy and unresolved decisions, use the [annotation guidelines](../docs/reference/annotation-guidelines.md), [project-author questions](../docs/registers/project-author-questions.md), [chief-editor questions](../docs/registers/chief-editor-questions.md), [tag questions](../docs/registers/tag-questions-for-chief-editor.md) and [data issues](../docs/registers/data-issues.md). Reports support those records; they do not silently resolve them. For a new import, follow the [current import guide](../docs/guides/primary-txt-import.md), not a historical repair plan or patch.

For new output, keep each investigation's narrative, inventories, previews and verification together in a descriptive series folder. Use `audits/` for audit/review series, `migrations/` for migration evidence, and `archive/` for completed or superseded process records. Retain unresolved/deferred findings as discoverable review material rather than archiving solely by age. Put reusable policies, conventions, designs and operating instructions in `docs/`, with links back to evidence here.

Before reusing any finding, check its recorded revision, fingerprints and scope against current data; historical line numbers and node addresses may have changed. Archival status does not make an unanswered question resolved. This index intentionally does not duplicate findings, decisions or issue statuses.
